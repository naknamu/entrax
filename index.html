FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Get in touch.

Is Nightingale affiliated with Kaplan?

No. Nightingale is an independent study resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kaplan, Inc. Kaplan® is a registered trademark of Kaplan, Inc. We do not use or reproduce Kaplan's questions.

Is the content based on the real exam?

Our sections follow the four areas the Kaplan Nursing Entrance Exam tests — Math, Reading, Writing, and Science — and the practice sets use a similar multiple-choice format. Every question is original content written for practice, not a real exam question.

How long does the Exam Pass last?

90 days from purchase. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription, so it will not renew automatically. You can retake any exam as often as you like during those 90 days.

What happens when my 90 days end?

Your account and history stay put — scores, explanations, and past attempts are still there. New graded attempts need an active pass, so you can extend for $9 and add another 90 days (counted from the later of today or your current expiry), or move to Lifetime for $49. Nothing is charged automatically.

What does Lifetime access mean?

It means access for as long as we operate Nightingale — not a guarantee that the Service will run forever, and not your own lifetime. If we ever permanently discontinue it, we will give notice and refund Lifetime purchases on a pro-rated basis over 24 months from the purchase date.

What can I use for free?

A full-length diagnostic test and sample practice sets, including your score and section breakdown. No credit card is required to create an account.

Can I practise on my phone?

Yes. The exam layout is mobile-first, attempts autosave, and you can resume on another device where you left off.

What if it isn't for me?

Once checkout opens, the Exam Pass, Extension, and Lifetime all come with a 14-day refund window. Details will be in the Terms before you pay.
