Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nightingale (“we”, “us”) collects when you use nightingaleexam.com (the “Service”), why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep this deliberately short and specific.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with a magic link we collect your email address. We may also store the display name you choose and a Firebase user ID. We do not ask for a password.

Practice data. When you take a practice exam we store your answers, the questions served, your score, the time you started and submitted, flagged questions, and the number of times the exam tab lost focus (used for exam-integrity display). This is what lets you review an attempt and see your progress.

Usage information. If analytics is enabled, we collect aggregated usage events such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, sign-up completion, and whether a practice test was completed. We configure analytics with IP anonymization and never send your name, email, or individual answers to analytics.

Technical information. Our hosting and database providers log standard request data (such as browser type, timestamps, and error information) to operate and secure the Service.

Support messages. If you email us, we keep the message and our reply so we can help you.

2. How we use your information

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to grade practice attempts, show explanations, and track your progress;
  • to operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • to understand which features are used and where the sign-up flow breaks;
  • to respond to support requests and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • to send service emails, such as magic links and, if you opt in, product updates.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your practice data for advertising.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract to provide the Service; legitimate interests to secure and improve it; consent for optional analytics and marketing emails; and legal obligation where we must retain records.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and local storage entries that are necessary for the Service to work — for example, to keep you signed in and to remember an in-progress attempt. These are not used for advertising.

If a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is configured, we also load Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (typically _ga and _ga_*). We request IP anonymization and do not send personal data. You can block these cookies in your browser, and we honour the browser's Do Not Track signal — when it is set, analytics is not loaded at all.

We do not run advertising pixels or sell audience data.

5. Who we share data with

We use service providers to run the Service. They process data on our behalf and are bound by their own data-processing terms:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database, hosting, and error monitoring;
  • Google Analytics — aggregate usage analytics, when enabled;
  • Stripe — payment processing, once paid passes are available. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we receive only a payment status and identifiers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a merger or acquisition. We will not sell your personal information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and practice data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and practice attempts within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons. Analytics data is retained according to the analytics provider's settings (currently 14 months).

7. Security

We use industry-standard providers and access controls, including authenticated access and database security rules that restrict each student to their own data. No system is completely secure, so please use a unique email account and keep access to it protected.

8. Children

The Service is intended for students preparing for a nursing entrance exam and is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 16, please use the Service only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without consent, we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email support@nightingaleexam.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. If a change is material, we will post a notice on the website or email registered users. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it changed.

12. Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@nightingaleexam.com or use the contact page.
