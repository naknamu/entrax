Study Guide

How to study for the Kaplan Nursing Entrance Exam

A focused, no-fluff plan for the four sections the exam tests: Math, Reading, Writing, and Science.

1

Take a diagnostic first

Before you drill, take a full-length timed mock. You need to know whether fractions or inferences are your real bottleneck.

2

Target your weakest category

Use your score breakdown to pick one area to improve each week. Small, focused beats broad and shallow.

3

Practice under real conditions

Use a timer, remove your phone, and don't look up answers mid-exam. Build stamina for the 2-hour block.

4

Review every explanation

When you miss a question, read the explanation before you move on. That is where the actual learning happens.

Test-day checklist

Simple prep the night before and morning of the exam.

Sleep on schedule

Aim for 7-8 hours. Cramming the night before usually hurts more than it helps.

Charge + bring ID

Bring a photo ID and arrive early. If the test is online, charge your device and find a quiet room.

Skip hard questions fast

Flag them and return later. The exam is timed, so don't let one problem eat five minutes.

Section-by-section tips

What the Kaplan Nursing Entrance Exam actually tests in each area, and what to prioritize.

Math

Focus on fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, and basic algebra. Word problems are common, so practice translating sentences into equations.

Reading

You will see short passages with main-idea, detail, inference, and organization questions. Read the question first, then scan the passage for the answer.

Writing

Expect grammar, sentence structure, and paragraph logic. If a choice makes the sentence harder to understand, it is usually wrong.

Science

Concentrate on basic chemistry and anatomy vocabulary. These questions test whether you recognize the meaning of key terms, not whether you can derive a formula.
