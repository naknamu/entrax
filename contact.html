What to include

  • Account or sign-in problem: the email address you use to sign in, and what happens when you request a magic link.
  • Billing or refund: the email address on the account and the approximate purchase date. Never send full card details.
  • Question or explanation issue: the exam title, the question text, and what looks wrong.
  • Data request: whether you want to access, export, or delete your data. We may ask you to verify control of the account email first.
